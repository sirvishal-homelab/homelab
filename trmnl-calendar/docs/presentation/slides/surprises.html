Lessons

What the real world taught us

Spike before you design

A short experiment showed the display server fetches the image during our request. That reshaped the whole push design.

Errors lie a little

A deleted screen came back as a 422, not a 404. The app now treats it as missing and recreates the screen.

Label estimates as estimates

The device reports battery voltage, not a percentage. We estimate the percentage and say so, with a history graph.

Judge on the real panel

Grey badges that looked fine on a monitor were hard to read on e-paper. Final tuning happened on the device.

These four are the most transferable points for a technical audience: verify an integration cheaply before committing to a design, do not trust error codes to mean what the documentation implies, be honest in the UI about what is measured and what is estimated, and test on the real target.
