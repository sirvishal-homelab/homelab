What it looks like

Week view: categories as lanes

Days across, categories down. Events, Work, Lunch and Dinner are rows you define.

Multi-day items draw as one bar, like the conference.

Holidays sit in a banner above the day.

Initials show who an item is for.

This image is generated by the app itself from made-up sample data, at the real 800 by 480 size, then shown here on a mock bezel. Today is the grey column. The four greys of the panel are used sparingly: grey for today and banners, black for text and rules.
