What it looks like

Edited from a phone

Phone first. Everyone edits in a browser; add it to the home screen and it behaves like an app.

Month page. Tap a day to see it, tap Add event to create one.

Filter by person to see just your own plans.

Forgiving. Unsent forms are kept as drafts, and a clash with someone else’s edit warns before overwriting.

No login. It is LAN only, by decision.

The month page exists only on the web: at 800 by 480 on the e-paper display a month grid would be too cramped to read. The phone screenshot uses made-up sample data. Two small features worth mentioning: drafts are saved in the browser so a lost connection does not lose a half-typed event, and the app detects when two people edited the same event.
