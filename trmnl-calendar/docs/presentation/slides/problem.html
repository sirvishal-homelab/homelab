The problem

A household plan that lives everywhere

Scattered

Appointments in two phones, work shifts in a roster, school dates in email, bin day on a fridge magnet.

Not glanceable

Nobody opens an app to ask what is on tomorrow. A screen on the wall answers before you ask.

Cloud by default

Most e-paper calendars want a vendor account and a hosted service. We wanted it on the home network.

Goal: one shared calendar, edited from any phone, shown on a quiet screen with no account and no cloud.

Keep this relatable: everyone has a family or team plan spread over several tools. The key requirement is the last card: local only. That choice drives most of the architecture later.
