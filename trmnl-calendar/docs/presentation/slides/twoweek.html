Same data, different picture. Views are saved presets, not separate calendars.

The user can build several views and switch which one is on the screen. This two-week list fits more days by giving up the lanes. The little boxed letters are the initials of the people an item is for. They went through a few rounds of tuning on the real panel: grey boxes were hard to read, so they are white with a black outline.
