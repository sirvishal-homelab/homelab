The constraints

A small, patient, grey screen

800×480

pixels on the TRMNL OG panel

4

shades of grey (2-bit)

10 min

between refreshes, to save battery

0

cloud accounts required

Everything we draw must survive being flattened to four greys and still read from across a room.

The device never talks to us directly. It asks Terminus, a self-hosted server that speaks the TRMNL protocol, for its next picture.

The device is a pull device: it wakes up, asks a server for an image, draws it, and goes back to sleep. That is what makes it last weeks on a battery, and it is why the refresh interval matters. Terminus is the open-source "bring your own server" option, so no TRMNL cloud account is needed.
