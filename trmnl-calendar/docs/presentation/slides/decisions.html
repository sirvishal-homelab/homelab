Decisions and trade-offs

Choices we wrote down

DecisionWhyTrade-off
Floating local timeOne household, one timezone. No daylight-saving bugs in stored data.Several timezones would need rework.
Day 31 clamps to month endA monthly repeat on the 31st lands on the 30th, as people expect.Differs from the calendar-file standard.
Images named by hashCache-friendly, and a free “did it change?” check.Old images need clearing out.
Soft delete with undoA slip on a phone is cheap to fix.Deleted items stay in the database.
One process, SQLiteSimple backups and no locking surprises.Does not scale out; we do not need it to.
Each row is a recorded decision. The point for this audience: we chose the constraint that fit one household and wrote down what it costs, so a future change of context (several timezones, several displays) is a deliberate step rather than a surprise.
