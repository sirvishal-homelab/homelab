Dark = working day. The pattern repeats forever from the start date.

Define the pattern once. One repeating event, not hundreds of entries.

Adjust single days. Often the in-or-out answer arrives the day before, so a day can be marked Out, In or Day off.

Undecided days simply say Work.

This feature replaced a planned calendar-file import. The owner’s partner works a repeating two-week pattern, so instead of importing someone else’s schedule we modelled the pattern itself: weeks A and B, a weekday checklist for each. Single days can then be overridden without touching the rule. The image on the right is the real form.
